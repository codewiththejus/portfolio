04 Design Philosophy

Precision, light and detail: spaces you can almost step into.

  1. i

    Space & precision

    Every scene starts from accurate drawings and honest proportions, a discipline shaped by working as a construction draftsman.

  2. ii

    Lighting

    Cove lines, pendants and warm glows are treated as architecture, never as an afterthought. Lighting mastery is the foundation of my work.

  3. iii

    Materials & colour

    Timber slats, textured walls, veined stone and soft sage or sand tones are tuned until each surface reads as real.

  4. iv

    Comfort

    Calm, warm and welcoming: clean lines and soft tones bring modern luxury and everyday comfort together.
